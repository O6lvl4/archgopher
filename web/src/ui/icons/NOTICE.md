# Icons

The icons stand for the service named next to them on every card and in the
catalog, and are used only for that. The providers' own icons are used
unchanged.

| Directory | Source | Terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `aws/` | [AWS Architecture Icons](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/icons/) (service icons, 48 px) | Provided by AWS for architecture diagrams |
| `azure/` | [Azure architecture icons](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/icons/) (V24) | Microsoft permits their use in architecture diagrams; they may not be cropped, flipped, rotated or distorted, and the service name appears near each icon |
| `gcp/` | [Google Cloud icons](https://cloud.google.com/icons) | Provided by Google for architecture diagrams |
| `cloudflare/`, `conoha/`, `general/` | [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) glyphs, recolored and placed on a tile: white on Cloudflare orange, white on ConoHa cyan, black on white for provider-neutral nodes | ISC License, Copyright (c) Lucide Icons and Contributors. `code`, `database` and `server` are derived from [Feather](https://feathericons.com): MIT License, Copyright (c) 2013-present Cole Bemis |

The full ISC and MIT notices are in
[web/public/licenses/lucide.txt](../../../public/licenses/lucide.txt), which the
build publishes next to the icons; each Lucide file names it in its first line.

The AWS, Azure and Google Cloud icons are trademarks of their owners and are
not covered by this repository's license. Cloudflare and ConoHa publish no
icon set for architecture diagrams (ConoHa's downloadable logos are for
articles introducing its services), so their resources use neutral glyphs on
a tile of a color, not their logos. The names Cloudflare and ConoHa are
trademarks of their owners and are used only to name their services.
